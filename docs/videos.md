# Short videos

Five short, silent videos of Foxfire Studio at work, each under a minute, with captions in the picture. The words of each one are written out under it too.

They were made on a test site with a made-up post and a stand-in microphone. Whisper's hearing is staged, with a skipped word, a filler and a misheard word built in; every screen and button is the plugin's own.

<figcaption><strong>Make your first reading.</strong> Open the studio on any post. It becomes a script, and nothing on the post changes. The Controls panel sets the pace, size and font, and switches recording on. Press Begin. After a short countdown, each word lights up as you read. Your voice is recorded as you go. Press Pause at any time, and the recording pauses too. Press Finish recording when you are done. The take is cleaned up, leveled for podcasts and made into an MP3, all in your browser. How it went: your pace, pauses and phrase endings, in plain words. Use this recording puts the reading on the post, with a player for your visitors. <a href="guide/first.html">Guide: Make your first reading</a></figcaption>
</figure>

<figcaption><strong>Read the review.</strong> After a take, or with Open a recording, Whisper listens back on your own computer. The review compares what you said with the script, word by word. A skipped word is struck through, a filler is marked, and a misheard word is underlined. Click any word to hear that moment of the recording. The timeline along the top shows your pace, with every flag on it. Each paragraph gets a score, and long pauses show how long they were. The tiles count each kind of slip. Click one to see just those moments. <a href="guide/review.html">Guide: Read the review</a></figcaption>
</figure>

<figcaption><strong>Edit by editing the script.</strong> In the review, turn on Edit audio. Now the script edits the recording. Tidy suggests cuts: fillers, repeats, and pauses longer than a second and a half. Keep the ones you agree with and apply them. To cut anything else, select the words and press Cut. Cuts join in the quiet between words. Undo steps back, and the original stays safe. Make edited MP3 builds the new file, with captions and chapters moved to match. <a href="guide/edit.html">Guide: Edit and polish a take</a></figcaption>
</figure>

<figcaption><strong>The player on a post.</strong> A post with a reading gets a listening player, in your own colours. Visitors press play, skip back or ahead, and change the speed. Read along lights up each word of the post as it is heard. Click any word in the post to jump to that moment. Transcript opens the whole text with times, for anyone who prefers to read. Listeners come back to where they stopped. No cookies, nothing sent elsewhere. <a href="guide/player.html">Guide: Put the player on a post</a></figcaption>

<figcaption><strong>Clips for social media.</strong> Each reading's page suggests moments for short clips, each a whole stretch of sentences. Make this clip opens the clip maker at that moment. Choose the shapes: square for feeds, vertical for Stories and Reels, wide for YouTube. Each clip gets its own tagged link, so you can see which one brought listeners. Make the clip draws it in your browser, captions and all (the making is sped up in the video).

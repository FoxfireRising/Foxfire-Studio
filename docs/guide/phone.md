# Record on a phone

*Phones and tablets can record every reading; long reviews are best left to a computer.*

Open the studio on the phone the same way as on a computer. The studio treats any touch-first device as a phone and keeps its work light so the browser does not close the page.

- Takes up to five minutes are reviewed on the phone by themselves, with the standard Whisper model.
- For a longer take the phone asks first. Send to my computer keeps the take on the site, private to you, and it opens in the studio on your computer for the full review. Try it here anyway reviews it on the phone.
- Phone take mode (Controls, Recording) sends each take to the site in pieces while you record, with a large level meter, so a closed page loses nothing. A take cut off partway can be carried on. A 10-minute take sends about 86 MB.
- With phone take mode on, the take waits for your computer. To review a short one on the phone itself, tap Make the MP3 here, then Try it here anyway at the bottom of the result box, and keep the page open until it finishes.
- Combine takes is only offered on a computer. With Settings, Take shelf switched on, the phone's short takes go on your shelf on the site too, so Combine takes on your computer can use them, and Continue on the phone picks up after a part read on a computer.

At most five takes can wait on the site for your computer at once, each for 14 days at most. When you open the studio on the computer, A take from your phone offers each one: Open it reviews it, Delete removes it. Foxfire Studio, Recordings, Sent from your phone lists what is waiting.

> Keep the room quiet and the phone close and steady. Check microphone (Controls, Practice) tells you how the room and your voice sound before you begin.

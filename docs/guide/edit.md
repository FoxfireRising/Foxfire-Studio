# Edit and polish a take

*Fix a stumble without reading the whole post again.*

## Cut and retake

1. In the full review, press Edit audio. Select words in the script to cut them (Whole sentence and Whole paragraph widen the selection), or use Tidy to have fillers, repeats and long pauses suggested, then Apply the ticked cuts.
2. To fix a sentence, select it and press Retake. Record it, then Hear it in place. The arrows beside it move each edge of the splice by 10 ms, and add or take away space filled with your room's own background. Use this take keeps it; Bring back the original undoes it.
3. Preview plays a few seconds around the selection, as it will sound. Undo takes back the last change.
4. Press Make edited MP3. Captions and chapters move to match the edits.
5. Check the edited version has Whisper listen again and flag anything new.

Editing works on a take made or opened in this tab since the page was loaded.

## Combine takes and Continue

Combine takes (at the top of the studio, beside Continue) builds one reading from several takes of the same post: choose which take reads each paragraph or sentence, or press Best of each to choose the take with the fewest flags for each paragraph, then Build the reading. Continue records the next part of a reading, starting one sentence before the first paragraph no take on the shelf has read, so a long post can be read in several sittings.

Both work from the post's take shelf: every take you review goes on it by itself, the newest six for 30 days. Clear the shelf empties it. The shelf is kept in your browser. With Settings, Take shelf switched on, it is kept on the site too and follows you to any computer or browser you sign in on: a take read on one shows on the others at once, and its recording comes from the site the first time Combine takes needs it there. Remove and Clear the shelf then work on every computer.

## A recording made elsewhere

Open a recording (at the top of the studio) takes a file you already have, cleans and levels it and runs the review, as if you had just read it. Use this recording then puts it on the post with captions.

# When something goes wrong

*Common problems and what usually fixes them.*

## In the studio

- The studio or player looks out of date after an update: empty the page cache, then press Ctrl+Shift+R in the studio (on a phone, close the tab and open the page again).
- The microphone was not available: the browser was not allowed to use it. Click the lock or settings icon beside the page's address, allow the microphone, and reload. On an iPhone: Settings, Apps, Safari, Microphone.
- The recording was very quiet: move closer to the microphone, or use a USB microphone. Check microphone (Controls, Practice) shows the level before you record.
- The script runs ahead of you: lower Pace (Controls, Reading), or switch on Follow my voice.
- Follow my voice turns itself off: the line under the script says why. Download voice following to this site in Settings, or close other tabs.
- A take is missing after a crash: open the studio for the same post again and choose Recover it.

## In the review

- The Whisper review stays on loading: the first load is large. Wait, or download Whisper to this site from Settings.
- Whisper did not hear enough: the take was very short or very quiet. Use Check microphone and record a longer stretch.
- Read words show grey as not read: Whisper heard a stretch wrongly. Review again with the larger model (Controls, Recording, Model).
- A reading in another language reviews badly: check the post's Reading language in its Reading audio box. Download the other-languages models to this site in Settings so they load quickly.
- The review was lost: it lives only in its tab, so a reload loses it. The take is still on the shelf; open it again from there.

## On a phone

- A phone closed the page during a review: open the studio again; the take is on the shelf. Next time use Send to my computer for long takes.
- Five takes are already waiting for your computer: open the studio on the computer and open or delete one (A take from your phone).
- The take shelf on the site is full: the take stays in the browser, and the studio says so. Delete old takes under Foxfire Studio, Recordings, or raise Space for every shelf in Settings, Take shelf.

## Clips, videos and collections

- A video stops partway: keep the tab in front and the computer awake until it finishes, and close other heavy tabs. Choose Smaller file for a long reading.
- A collection will not join a reading: the reading must be a constant-bitrate MP3 on this site. Make it again in the studio, or leave it out.
- The audiobook check fails on a chapter with the music bed: make that chapter's audiobook copy from the review (Finish for, Keep as this post's audiobook chapter).

## On the site

- No listens appear: analytics is off, or you tested while logged in, or your browser sends Do Not Track.
- Every listener comes from the same place: the site is behind a proxy such as Cloudflare. List it under Settings, Listener analytics, Trusted proxies.
- A directory turns the feed down: look at Ready for directories? on Podcast feed. The cover art's size and the owner email are the usual causes.
- Check private folders reports a folder as open: the web server is not reading the folder's own rules. Ask your host to allow .htaccess files in uploads, or to block those folders another way.
- Players look unstyled or the page shows old pictures after a change: empty the page cache and check the page while logged out.

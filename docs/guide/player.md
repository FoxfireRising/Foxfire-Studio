# Put the player on a post

*Where visitors hear your readings, and how to choose where it sits.*

Use this recording puts a reading on the post. You can also upload an MP3 yourself and choose it in the post's Reading audio box, at the side of the post editor.

Settings, Listening player, Show the player to chooses Nobody, Only logged-in editors (a preview while you get ready), or Everyone. Where decides where it appears:

- At the top of the post, unless the post or its template places it.
- Only where I put it: add the Reading player block in the editor, or the [foxfire_player] shortcode.

Listeners get skips, speed, chapters at the post's headings, a transcript, links that start at a moment (add ?t=1:45 to a post's address), and lock screen controls on phones. Under Features you can switch on Read along (the post's text lights up as it is read, when the post has captions), offer to resume where a listener left off, and show a Download button. Buttons sets the skip lengths and the speeds. Show name is what podcast apps and lock screens show as the show.

Settings, Readability checks that the player's colours are easy to read, and Make it readable fixes any that are not.

## Taking a reading off a post

To take a reading off a post without losing it, open the reading under Foxfire Studio, Readings and use Take off the post. It is kept aside, private, until you put it back or delete it for good (see Recordings and waiting takes).

> After any change, empty your page cache and check the post while logged out.

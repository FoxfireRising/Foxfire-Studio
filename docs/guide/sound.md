# Finish the sound

*How each take is cleaned, leveled and made into an MP3, and how to hear the difference.*

Every take is finished the same way before it becomes an MP3, in the reader's own browser. The Whisper review always listens to the untouched recording, so none of this changes its results. The site's choices are in Settings, under Broadcast sound and Music bed.

## Finish for

Settings, Broadcast sound, Finish for chooses where readings are going. Podcast, mono is the usual choice: -19 LUFS with peaks under -1 dBTP, as a mono MP3 at 128 kbps, which plays as loud as a -16 LUFS stereo show at half the file size. The other presets are Podcast, stereo; Spotify and YouTube; Broadcast (EBU R128); Audiobook (ACX); Simple leveling; and Custom, which lets you set Loudness, True peak limit and Channels yourself.

Steps sets the cleanup the presets share: a low rumble filter, and how strongly pops on P and B, harsh S sounds, breaths and loud words are softened (Off, Gentle, Standard or Strong). Standard is what every version before 1.13.0 made.

## Background noise

Background noise reduction has two engines. Standard (RNNoise) is quick. Stronger (DeepFilterNet 3) also takes out sounds with a shape, such as a bell, a creaking chair or mouth sounds, and takes about twice as long; with it, the Strong amount usually sounds best. If a device is too slow for Stronger, that take uses Standard and the review's Room line says so. It all applies when Clean up audio is ticked in the studio (Controls, Recording). If words start to sound watery or their endings get cut, choose a gentler amount.

## Voice polish

- Slow leveling evens out a level that drifts over minutes, by 4 dB at most (2 dB in verse and ritual).
- Lower clicks between words softens mouth clicks shorter than 15 ms, using Whisper's word times. They are lowered, never cut.
- EQ for each setup suggests a gentle EQ for each device and microphone from its first three takes.

Polish shapes new takes, Use this recording, clips and Share for feedback. The Original download stays as recorded, and readings already on posts stay as they are until you make them again.

## Hear the polish

In the review, Hear the polish plays one sentence before and after, and can try other strengths, the other engine, the EQ and click lowering on that take only. Use for this take keeps a choice for this take; Back to the Settings choice returns to the site's. It is the quickest way to decide a setting before changing it for everyone.

## The music bed

A music bed is quiet music under a reading: it plays alone for a few seconds, dips before the first word, stays low under the voice, then rises after the last word and fades out. It is mixed in last, after any editing, so the review hears the voice alone and the Original download stays voice only.

1. In Settings, Music bed, press Add tracks and choose MP3, WAV, OGG or M4A files from the Media Library. Add a credit if the music needs one. Use only music you own or have a licence for.
2. Choose a Default track, or leave No music bed unless a post chooses one.
3. Each post can choose its own track, or none, in its Reading audio box. In the studio, Listen with music plays the take with it before you use it.

Under the voice sets how far below the voice it sits (Standard is 20 dB). A track shorter than the reading loops with a smooth crossfade. The credit goes into the MP3 and the podcast episode's notes. A reading with the music bed never gets a sponsor message in the middle, and is not suited to an audiobook.

## An audiobook copy

With Keep an audiobook copy ticked (Settings, Broadcast sound), Use this recording also keeps a voice-only copy finished for ACX, for the collections' Audiobook box. It takes about 1.4 MB a minute in a private folder. A reading already on its post can get one from the review: Finish for, then Keep as this post's audiobook chapter.

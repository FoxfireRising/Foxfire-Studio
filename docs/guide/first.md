# Make your first reading

*From a post to a finished MP3 on that post, in one sitting.*

Before the first reading, an administrator sets up the plugin once with the setup wizard. It asks about the look, the player, privacy and files, and ends with a practice reading. It can be run again at any time from Settings, Run setup again.

1. Open the studio: in the Posts list, hover over a post and click Teleprompter (it opens in a new tab), or go to Tools, Teleprompter and pick a post. Foxfire Studio, Readings to make also has a Start button beside each post that has no reading yet.
2. Try the practice reading first: a one-minute script that belongs to no post. The first time, click Try it under New here, and it checks your microphone first. Any time after, open it from the Controls panel, under the Practice heading, with Practice reading, or from Open the practice reading on the studio's first screen or on Foxfire Studio, Your progress.
3. In the Controls panel (press Controls at the top if it is closed), under the Recording heading, make sure Record my voice, Clean up audio and Whisper review are ticked.
4. Press Begin (or Space) and read. Each word lights up as you go. Press Finish recording when you reach the end. With Follow my voice off, the script stops at its last word and the recording keeps going until you press Finish recording.
5. The dialog Your reading is ready shows a short read-out of your pace and pauses. Choose Open full review to see every slip Whisper heard.
6. When you are happy with the take, press Use this recording. The MP3 and its captions go on the post, and the post shows a listening player.

Nothing is lost if a take is not right: it stays on the post's take shelf, and the next one goes beside it. Read the post again, or fix just the stumbles (see Edit and polish a take).

> Takes are saved in your browser as you record, so a crash or a closed tab never loses one. Reopen the studio for the same post and choose Recover it.

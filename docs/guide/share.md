# Share for feedback

*Let a friend hear a take with the script and leave comments on it.*

Share for feedback (in the review) lets someone with an account on your site hear a take with the script lighting up as it plays, and leave comments pinned to a moment or a word. A friend without an account can be added as a Subscriber (Users, Add New User, Role: Subscriber); Subscribers see nothing else of the plugin.

1. In the review, press Share for feedback and choose who may hear it.
2. They get an email. They sign in, listen, leave comments, and press Done.
3. You get one email for each visit, when they press Done (or an hour after their last comment). The comments show in the review and on Foxfire Studio, Shared takes.

Editors and administrators can share with anyone on the site. Authors and contributors can share with the site's editors and administrators, and with anyone who has shared a take with them.

A share lasts 14 days, or until you end it. Then its audio is deleted from the site and the people you chose can no longer open it; their comments stay.

# What listeners get

*The player as your visitors meet it, including with a keyboard or a screen reader.*

- Play, skip back and ahead, and speed, with a keyboard as well as a mouse or a finger. The buttons are named for screen readers, and a new speed is read out.
- Chapters at the post's headings, and links that start at a moment.
- Transcript, on every reading with captions: every line as a button that plays from there, the line being heard marked.
- Read along, where switched on: the post's own text lights up as it is read.
- Lock screen and headphone controls on phones, and resume where they left off, remembered in their own browser only.
- Only one thing plays at a time: starting one reading, or a YouTube or SoundCloud player, pauses the others.

Settings, Readability checks every colour pair the player uses against WCAG 2.2 AA, and Make it readable fixes only those that fall short. The studio itself works without a mouse too (see Keyboard shortcuts).

A reading without captions has no Transcript or Read along. Give your readings captions (see Captions and chapters) so deaf and hard of hearing visitors, and anyone in a quiet room, can follow.

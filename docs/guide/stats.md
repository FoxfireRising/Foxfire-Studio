# Listening numbers and reports

*What listeners do with each reading, measured on your own site.*

Listener analytics is off on new sites. Add the paragraph from Settings, For your privacy policy to your policy first, then tick Measure listening under Settings, Listener analytics. Each screen has a Period menu at the top, and What these numbers mean at the bottom.

> Your own visits are left out. To test, use a logged-out browser without Do Not Track, play for a few seconds, then close the tab.

## Overview

Plays, how many visitors pressed play, how many were still listening at one minute and heard to the end, average speed and listening time, each compared with the period before. Below them: plays each day, top readings and sources, how visitors read or listen, and how long they take to press play. Changes lets you mark a change on a date (a new cover, a new microphone) so it shows as a flag on the charts. Raw data downloads every visit as CSV.

## Readings

Every reading with its plays and how much was heard. Click one for its own page: its listening curve, with the sentence that was playing where listeners leave, where its listeners came from, how they listened, the take it came from, and the clip maker and full video.

## Sources and the link builder

Channels and campaigns that bring listeners, with how many pressed play and heard to the end; that matters more than clicks. Channels with fewer than 30 plays are too small to compare. The Link builder makes a tagged link for each place you share a reading (choose the post, the channel, an optional campaign and a start time, then Copy link), so each one shows up as its own source.

## Insights

Plain sentences about what lines up with readings heard to the end: length, tempo, tags, the day and time published, where listeners came from, and your delivery. Each stays quiet until there is enough to go on, usually six readings with at least 30 plays each, and says what it still needs. Your reading, below, works from your own full takes once you have five.

## Reports

An email with the same numbers as the dashboard, totals only. Under Reports, tick Send the report by email on a schedule, choose How often (monthly by default, or weekly, every two weeks or every quarter), the time of day, who it goes to (up to 10 addresses) and what to include. Preview shows it as on a computer or a phone; Send now sends one at once without changing the schedule. Past reports keeps the archive.

## More switches

Under Settings, Listener analytics: Leave out chooses whose visits are not counted; Privacy signals respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control; Returning listeners tells new listeners from returning ones (it needs a policy line first); Google Analytics also sends plays to a Google Analytics already on the site; Keep sets how long visits are kept; Trusted proxies is for a site behind a CDN. Listener countries can download a free country database so countries show. Recent listens confirms that listens arrive.

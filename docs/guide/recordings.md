# Recordings and waiting takes

*Every reading a post has had, readings kept aside, and takes waiting for your computer.*

Foxfire Studio, Recordings has up to four lists.

## Every recording

Every MP3 that has been a post's reading, with its length, size, bitrate, tempo, when it went live, whether it is on the post now, whether it was edited, and how it got there. Download CSV saves the list.

## Kept aside

Readings taken off their post with Take off the post (on the reading's page under Foxfire Studio, Readings). Each is kept in a folder visitors cannot open, and can be played here. Put back returns it to the Media Library at the address it had, with its captions, so it is the same episode in the podcast feed and its listening history carries on. Delete for good removes it after asking.

## Sent from your phone

Takes recorded on a phone in phone take mode, or sent with Send to my computer, waiting to be reviewed on a computer. At most five can wait at once. Each is deleted from the site once you use, download or delete it in the studio, and after 14 days at most. To make room, open the studio on your computer: A take from your phone offers each waiting take with Open it and Delete.

## Your take shelf on the site

Shown while Settings, Take shelf is on: the takes of yours kept on the site, by post, with Delete for each. Only you can see or open your own. Takes leave by themselves after 30 days, and each post keeps its newest six.

> Shared takes are listed on their own screen, Foxfire Studio, Shared takes (see Share for feedback).

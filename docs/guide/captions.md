# Captions and chapters

*Where a reading's captions and chapters come from, and how to fix or add them.*

## Captions

The review makes a reading's captions from the post's own words, timed by what Whisper heard: a line for each short sentence, longer sentences split every dozen words or so. Use this recording puts them on the post with the MP3. If the review has not finished when you press it, press Use this recording again once it has, to add the captions. After an edit, words that were cut leave the captions too.

Captions give the player its Transcript button and Read along, give the podcast episode its transcript, and give clips their words. A reading without them shows No captions yet on Foxfire Studio, Readings.

## Fixing a caption

- A wrong word in the captions is a wrong word in the post, since captions use the post's text. Fix the post, then record the reading again or open the same recording with Open a recording, and press Use this recording.
- A line that starts too early or too late usually means Whisper missed a stretch. Review the take again with the larger model (Controls, Recording, Model, Larger).
- For a small fix without a new review, download the captions file (the review's Captions (.vtt) button, or the Media Library), correct it in any plain text editor, upload it, and choose it in the post's Reading audio box under Captions for read along.

## Chapters

Headings in the post become chapters. They are written inside the MP3 itself, so they need no file of their own: the player lists them, podcast apps that show chapters show them, and a full video's description gets YouTube chapter times. A collection gets one chapter for each reading, with each reading's own headings inside it.

## A reading you recorded somewhere else

In the post's Reading audio box, Reading (MP3), press Choose and pick the MP3 from the Media Library. Add its captions file under Captions for read along if you have one. To have Foxfire Studio make captions for it, open the studio on the post, press Open a recording, choose the file, let the review finish, and press Use this recording. Read by adds a credit such as read by Robin under the player and in the feed.

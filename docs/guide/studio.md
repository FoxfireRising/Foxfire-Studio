# Tools for reading

*Ways to make reading aloud easier before you press Begin.*

Each of these lives in the studio's Controls panel unless it says otherwise. Most controls there have a ? button beside them that explains them in a sentence or two.

## Follow my voice and timed pace

Under Controls, Reading, Follow my voice moves the highlight as you speak, with a small speech model on your own device; your voice never leaves the browser. It follows English readings only for now, and timed pace takes over if the device cannot keep up.

With Follow my voice off, the script moves at Pace (words a minute) and does not wait for you: pause at each full stop, or lower Pace. Left and right arrow keys change it while you read. Size and Width change the script's text, Line sets how far down the screen the reading line sits, and Breathe between paragraphs adds a short stop at the end of each paragraph. Every person's choices are kept in their own browser; Settings, Reading holds the starting values.

## Cues

1. Open the studio on the post. Under Controls, Practice, switch on Cues.
2. With the script paused, click a word. Choose a pause or a breath after it, emphasis on it, or Let this line fade for a line meant to trail off.
3. The marks show every time the post is read, for anyone. The post's own text never changes.

## Pronunciation notes

A pronunciation note shows a short hint, such as SOW-in, above a word while you read. To add one, switch on Cues as above, click the word and choose Add a note. Choose whether it belongs to every post or just this one.

Foxfire Studio, Pronunciation lists every note and every word taught to Whisper, across all posts. Words that may need a note lists names and long words from your published posts that have no note yet, posts still to read first; Add a note there and it shows the first time you read them, or choose Not needed. Click a note to change it. Export CSV, Export backup and Import move the list to another site. A studio tab that is already open shows changes made there once it is reloaded (Ctrl+Shift+R).

## Prompter view

Prompter view (Controls, Prompter view, Open prompter view, or the P key) shows only the script, full screen, for a glass teleprompter rig or a screen across the room. Mirror flips it to read the right way round in the glass (most rigs need Left to right), Shift moves it over the camera lens, Fade above and below keeps your eyes near the lens, and Bright text on black reflects most clearly. Leave prompter view, or Escape, goes back.

## Practice and drills

- Warm up: about three minutes of breathing, tongue twisters and pacing.
- Check microphone: a ten-second test of the room and your voice level before you record.
- Practice reading: the one-minute script that belongs to no post. Its takes never go on a post; Your progress lists them as A practice reading.
- Opening drill: read just the opening, again and again, and compare each take with the last.
- Focus: one habit to work on. The review leads with it and lists its flags first.
- Kind: the kind of reading for this post (see Kinds of reading).

## Readings to make

Foxfire Studio, Readings to make lists your published posts that have no reading yet, newest first, with each one's length in words, how long it would run at your pace, how hard it looks, and its takes so far. Click a column to sort. Start opens the studio on the post in a new tab; Hide takes it off the list.

## Your progress and goals

Foxfire Studio, Your progress shows every take you have made over time, with its pace, accuracy and delivery, your personal bests and a practice log. Under Goals, Set goals lets you choose one to three targets, such as pace at least 135 and at most 150, fillers at most 3, or room quiet at least 30. Leave either box empty for a goal in one direction only. Every take is then checked against your goals here and in the studio's read-out after each take.

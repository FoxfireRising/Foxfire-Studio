# Who can do what

*Which WordPress roles can record, see the numbers and change settings.*

- Administrators: everything, including Settings, Podcast feed, Reports, Sponsor messages, the setup wizard and every listening number.
- Editors: the studio on any post, Use this recording, Your progress (Overview opens it for them), Insights about their own reading, Pronunciation, Shared takes, Collections and Help. They can share a take with anyone on the site.
- Authors: the same on their own posts and collections. They share takes with editors and administrators.
- Contributors: the studio and its review on their own drafts, Your progress, Pronunciation, Shared takes and Help. Putting a reading on a post needs the right to upload files, which contributors do not have: they download the MP3, or share the take with an editor who can put it on.
- Subscribers: none of the plugin's screens. They can hear a take shared with them, and can be members with a feed of their own (see Member feeds).

Overview, Readings, Readings to make, Sources, Recordings and the listener half of Insights show numbers and readings across the whole site, so they are for administrators. A developer can widen this, and who may make sponsor messages or share with anyone, with the filters rrt_dashboard_cap, rrt_spot_capability and rrt_share_anyone_capability.

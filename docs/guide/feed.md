# The podcast feed

*Every reading as a podcast episode, for Apple Podcasts, Spotify and other apps.*

Every published post with a reading becomes an episode, newest first. The feed is off until you publish it. Foxfire Studio, Podcast feed holds the show's details and checks the feed is ready for the directories. Podcast apps fetch readings straight from the site, so those plays are not in the listening numbers; Count downloads counts them instead (see Download counting and storage offload).

## Publishing the feed

1. Fill in the show: Show title (left empty, the Show name from Settings is used), Description, Cover art (a square JPEG or PNG, 1400 to 3000 pixels wide, ideally under 512 KB), Author, Owner email (public, and where Apple and Spotify send their codes), Category, Content and Copyright. Press Save.
2. Fix anything listed under Ready for directories?
3. Tick Publish the podcast feed and save.
4. Empty your page cache, then open the feed's address while logged out to check it.
5. Submit the address to Apple Podcasts Connect and Spotify for Creators. They check it and list the show within a few days.

Keep Lock ticked unless you are moving the show to a podcast host yourself: it asks other hosts not to import the show.

## More for podcast apps

Apps that read the Podcasting 2.0 tags show these; the rest ignore them. Host names the person on the show, with a link and picture. Support link adds a button such as a donation page, with your own words. Place says where the show is made or what it is about. Text lines holds codes a directory may ask for to prove the show is yours. Each episode also gets its chapters, its transcript and a soundbite (the stretch chosen with Use for the feed on the reading's page, else the top suggested clip).

Pings tells Podcast Index and the WebSub hub when a new reading is in the feed, so apps fetch it within minutes. Only the feed's address is sent, at most once every 10 minutes. It needs a line in your privacy policy first.

## Collections in the feed

A published collection with In the podcast feed ticked is an episode too, as a Bonus (its readings are also episodes of their own) or Full. See Collections and audiobooks.

On the same screen: Member feeds, Sponsor messages, Storage offload, Download counting and Private folders, each with a guide of its own.

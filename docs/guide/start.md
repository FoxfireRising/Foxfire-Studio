# Start here

*Which guides to read first, for the person who runs the site and for the person who reads aloud.*

Foxfire Studio has two kinds of user: the person who looks after the site, and the person who reads posts aloud. Often they are the same person wearing two hats. Pick the path that fits what you want to do today.

## If you look after the site

Begin with the setup wizard: Foxfire Studio, Set up, while it is offered, or Settings, Run setup again at any time. It covers the choices that matter on the first day in about five minutes, and nothing changes unless you change it. Then read these, in this order:

- [Settings, section by section](settings.md): What each part of Foxfire Studio's Settings screen does.
- [Put the player on a post](player.md): Where visitors hear your readings, and how to choose where it sits.
- [Privacy and your privacy policy](privacy.md): What stays in your browser, what reaches your site, and which switches need a policy line first.
- [Who can do what](roles.md): Which WordPress roles can record, see the numbers and change settings.
- [The podcast feed](feed.md): Every reading as a podcast episode, for Apple Podcasts, Spotify and other apps.
- [Update, back up or remove the plugin](upkeep.md): Installing a new version, what to back up, and what removing it deletes.

## If you read aloud

Begin with the practice reading: a one-minute script that belongs to no post, so nothing you do there can go wrong. Then read these, in this order:

- [Make your first reading](first.md): From a post to a finished MP3 on that post, in one sitting.
- [Tools for reading](studio.md): Ways to make reading aloud easier before you press Begin.
- [Read the review](review.md): What the numbers and flags mean, and what to do about them.
- [Edit and polish a take](edit.md): Fix a stumble without reading the whole post again.
- [Record on a phone](phone.md): Phones and tablets can record every reading; long reviews are best left to a computer.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](keys.md): The studio can be used without a mouse.

## Words used in every guide

- The studio (also called the teleprompter) is the page where you read a post aloud and record it. It opens in a tab of its own.
- The Controls panel is the panel beside the script in the studio (at the bottom on a phone). Its headings, such as Reading, Recording and Practice, group its controls, and the guides name them in that order: Controls, then the heading, then the control.
- A take is one recording of a post. Every take you review goes on that post's take shelf for 30 days, so you can come back to it.
- The review is what Whisper, a speech model that runs in your own browser, heard in a take, word by word, with flags on the moments worth a listen.
- A reading is the MP3 that is on a post, usually with its captions. Visitors hear it in the listening player on the post, and podcast apps get it from the feed.
- The Reading audio box is the box at the side of each post's editor that holds its reading, captions, kind of reading, language and who can hear it.

# Privacy and your privacy policy

*What stays in your browser, what reaches your site, and which switches need a policy line first.*

Recording, cleanup, the MP3 and the Whisper review all happen in the reader's own browser. A recording reaches your site only when the reader presses Use this recording, shares a take, sends a phone take to their computer, or reviews a take while Settings, Take shelf is on. The plugin sends nothing to its authors.

These are off until you switch them on, and each needs a line in your privacy policy first:

- Listener analytics, and Returning listeners with it (Settings).
- Google Analytics events (Settings).
- Download counting, Measurement prefixes and Pings (Podcast feed).
- Feed activity for member feeds (Podcast feed).
- Embedded players (Settings).

Settings, Take shelf is off on new sites too. When it is on, each person's reviewed takes are kept on the site, where only they can open them, for 30 days at most. Settings, Embedded players is off as well. When it is on, visitors contact YouTube or SoundCloud only when they press play; until then the site shows its own copy of the picture.

Settings, For your privacy policy gives the wording to copy. To make no outside requests at all, download Whisper, voice following, the fonts, the MP3 encoder and the noise model to your site from Settings. Readers can ask for their own stats to be exported or erased with WordPress's personal data tools (Tools, Export Personal Data and Erase Personal Data).

## Private folders

Readings for members, readings kept aside, takes sent from a phone, shared takes, take shelves and audiobook copies are kept in folders in uploads that visitors are turned away from. Foxfire Studio, Podcast feed, Private folders, Check private folders puts a small test file in each and asks for it the way a visitor would, then deletes the test files. Run it after moving the site or changing its server or security plugin.

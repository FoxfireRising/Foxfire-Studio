# Settings, section by section

*What each part of Foxfire Studio's Settings screen does.*

Settings is under Foxfire Studio, Settings (and Settings, Foxfire Studio in WordPress's own menu). The main form's choices are saved with Save settings at its bottom; Take shelf and Embedded players have save buttons of their own. Each person's own studio choices are kept in their browser; these are the starting values. Each section's heading on the screen has a Help link to its part below.

## Look, wording, colours and fonts

Look starts from a preset (Neutral or Dark), which replaces the wording, colours and fonts. Wording holds the words the studio and player show, such as the name in the control bar and the label on the player. Teleprompter look chooses the Foxfire Studio artwork or your WordPress theme's colours, and an optional Intro video for the studio. Colours sets the player's and studio's colours; Readability, under them, checks every pair against WCAG 2.2 AA, and Make it readable fixes only the ones that fall short. Fonts sets the reading, heading and button fonts and an optional Google Fonts link.

## Reading

Starting values for pace, text size, column width and the reading line; Pace by kind of reading (see Kinds of reading); which studio switches start on; and Content that can be read: posts, pages, collections and sponsor messages.

## Broadcast sound

How each reading is cleaned, leveled and made into an MP3: Finish for, Steps, Voice polish, Background noise reduction and Audiobook copy. See Finish the sound.

## Music bed

Tracks to play quietly under readings, and how far under the voice. See Finish the sound, The music bed.

## Whisper and Whisper on this site

This browser says what the browser in use can do. Load Whisper from chooses this site or Hugging Face. Whisper on this site downloads the standard model (about 116 MB) to your hosting, so reviews load from your site; The larger model (about 570 MB) and Other languages (about 650 MB) can be downloaded the same way. Keep the page open while each download runs. Whisper always runs on the reader's own computer either way.

## Listening player

Who sees the player, where it goes, extra custom fields to look for a reading or a Read by credit in, the skip and speed buttons, Read along, resume and Download, and the Show name. See Put the player on a post.

## Reader stats

Keep stats on this site keeps each take's numbers, the words you have taught Whisper and a record of each post's reading on the site, so your history follows you to any computer. Only numbers are kept, never recordings. When the plugin is deleted decides whether everything else goes too (see Update, back up or remove the plugin).

## Listener analytics, countries and recent listens

Measure listening and its options (see Listening numbers and reports), Listener countries, and Recent listens, a quick check that listens arrive.

## Take shelf

Keep each person's take shelf on the site, so it follows them to any computer or browser, with Space for every shelf in MB. When the space is used up, new takes stay in the browser and the studio says so. Delete every shelf on the site removes them all. Press Save take shelf settings.

## Embedded players

The two switches that make YouTube and SoundCloud players wait for play. See YouTube and SoundCloud players.

## Voice following, fonts, encoder and noise model on this site

Voice following on this site downloads Follow my voice's model (about 45 MB). Fonts, MP3 encoder and noise model on this site downloads copies of those too, so the studio makes no outside requests apart from Whisper and voice following when their files are not here. After changing the font link, save, then download again.

# Browsers and phones

*What works where.*

- Reading, timed pace, recording, the MP3 and the Whisper review work in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on computers and phones.
- Clips and full videos work in all four on a computer, and in Safari on an iPhone or iPad.
- The larger Whisper model runs on the graphics chip where the browser offers WebGPU, as current Chrome, Edge and Safari do. When it would be slower than the take, Automatic uses the standard model instead.
- Follow my voice is tested in Chrome; elsewhere timed pace takes over if the device cannot keep up. It follows English readings only for now.
- Chinese, Japanese, Thai and the other languages written without spaces between words are not offered as reading languages yet.

Settings (under Whisper) and the studio (Controls, under Recording) both show a This browser line that says what the browser in use can do: whether it makes clips and videos, where Whisper runs, and whether its stored takes could be cleared.

Every browser keeps its own takes, settings and taught words. Chrome and Firefox on the same computer are as separate as two computers. Settings, Reader stats keeps each take's numbers on the site, and Settings, Take shelf keeps the takes themselves there, so either one follows you from browser to browser.

> On a computer with two graphics chips, choose the faster one for your browser in the system's graphics settings. On Windows: Settings, System, Display, Graphics, choose the browser, set it to High performance, then close the browser fully and open it again.

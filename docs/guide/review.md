# Read the review

*What the numbers and flags mean, and what to do about them.*

Whisper, a speech model that runs in your own browser, listens to the take and compares it with the script word by word. Nothing is sent anywhere while it does. The review opens from Open full review in the dialog Your reading is ready.

## The numbers and flags

- The first number is the share of the script's words Whisper heard as written. A few missed words are often Whisper mishearing a name, so listen before you read again.
- Flags mark moments worth a listen: a skipped or changed word, a long pause, a rushed or slow stretch, a stray sound. Click a flag or a word to hear that moment.
- Pace is words a minute. In English, between about 115 and 165 is a comfortable listening pace; a post with a kind of reading, such as verse, is judged against that kind's own range. In other languages the pace is a number until you have three takes in that language, then it is judged against your own.
- When Whisper mishears a word you said correctly, click I said it right on it. It remembers, in this post and in every other.

## The parts of the review

- At a glance: the headline numbers for this take, and your goals if you set any.
- How it sounded: a line each on the room, the pauses, how your sentences end, energy and pitch, and pace when there is a kind of reading to judge it by. With a Focus chosen, its line comes first.
- Pause map: every pause, long ones marked, so you can see where the reading breathes.
- Flagged moments: each flag with the words Whisper heard, in order. Work down the list.
- Words Whisper has learned: the words taught with I said it right, for every post or just this one.
- Your takes: this post's takes side by side (accuracy, pace, slips, clicks, sentences to retake), so you can see whether the last one was better.
- Reading the marks: what each colour and mark in the script means.

## Which Whisper model

Under Controls, Recording, Model, Automatic picks the larger Whisper model where the computer's graphics chip is fast enough and the standard model elsewhere. Choose Standard or Larger to decide for yourself. The larger model hears names, unusual words and quiet endings better and takes longer on a computer without a fast graphics chip.

Whisper hears each reading in its own language. A post is read in the site's language unless you choose another in its Reading audio box, under Reading language. Readings in any language but English use Whisper's multilingual models; English readings keep the English ones. Words taught to Whisper are kept for each language separately.

## Downloads from the review

- Download MP3: the latest MP3 of this take (the edited one once it is made, with the music bed when that is switched on).
- Captions (.vtt): the captions file, timed to this take.
- Finish for: a one-off version finished for another place, such as an audiobook copy, without changing the site's choice.
- Use this recording: puts the MP3 and its captions on the post as its reading.

> The review lives only in its tab. Reloading the page loses it, though the take itself stays on the shelf.

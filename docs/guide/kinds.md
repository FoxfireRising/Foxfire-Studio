# Kinds of reading

*Prose, story, verse, and ritual and chant: what each one changes.*

Each post can be given a kind of reading. The studio then starts in the middle of that kind's pace range, and the review judges the pace of a take against it, so a slow ritual is never flagged as too slow for prose.

- Prose: 140 to 160 words a minute.
- Story: 130 to 150 words a minute.
- Verse: 110 to 135 words a minute. A line that trails off is expected, so only a deep fade is flagged, and slow leveling moves the level by 2 dB at most.
- Ritual and chant: 95 to 120 words a minute, treated like verse for fades and leveling.

Choose the kind in the post's Reading audio box, under Kind of reading, or in the studio under Controls, Practice, Kind; either saves it with the post. A post with no kind uses the starting pace from Settings, Reading, and its review uses the comfortable English range of about 115 to 165.

The ranges above are the starting ones. Settings, Reading, Pace by kind of reading changes them for the whole site. Sponsor messages can also be kept out of some kinds (Podcast feed, Sponsor messages, Mid-rolls in).

# Make a clip or a full video

*Short captioned clips for social media, and the whole reading as a video for YouTube or Spotify.*

Both are made in your browser from the reading already on the post. Go to Foxfire Studio, Readings and click a reading.

## A clip

1. Press Make a clip, or pick one of the Suggested clips: the opening and the three strongest moments, with why each was chosen. The play button beside one plays just that stretch.
2. Choose the moment, tick the shapes (Square, Vertical, Wide), and press Make the clip.
3. Watch it before you post it. The words on the picture come from the reading's captions.

Use for the feed on a suggestion makes it the episode's soundbite in the podcast feed. A clip can also be made straight from a take's review (Make a clip), before the take goes on the post.

## The full video

1. Further down the reading's page, find Full-length video. Choose a picture (the post's featured image, the show's cover art, the look's colours, or any picture from the Media Library), whether to put the captions on it, and the quality.
2. Press Make the video and keep the tab open until it finishes. The page shows the time left, and Cancel stops it.
3. Download it, with the description (with chapter times when the reading allows), the captions file and a thumbnail for the upload.

> On a phone the full video goes straight to Files, Downloads (or your browser's downloads); the page shows no player for it.

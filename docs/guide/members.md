# Member feeds

*A private podcast feed for each member, with readings only members can hear.*

Member feeds give chosen people a feed of their own, with the readings for members alongside the rest. They work whether or not the public feed is published.

## Setting it up

1. Go to Foxfire Studio, Podcast feed, Member feeds, tick Give members their own feed, and choose Who is a member by role. Administrators always have a feed, so you can check yours. Each person's profile (Users, Edit) can also say Always or Never.
2. Optionally give the feed a title and a Become a member link, shown under a reading for members and in a lapsed member's feed. Save.
3. For each reading, choose Who can hear this reading in its Reading audio box: Everyone, Members first, then everyone (with Days for members first), or Members only. Collections have the same choice.

A reading for members shows a short message to visitors who may not hear it. Its files move to a private folder while it is for members, and come back when it opens to everyone.

## What a member does

Each member finds their own address on their profile (Users, Profile, under Your members podcast feed), or on any page where you place the [foxfire_member_feed] shortcode. On Podcast feed, the list Members with a feed has Email them their feed address. Then, in their app:

- Apple Podcasts on an iPhone or iPad: Library, then the ... menu, then Follow a Show by URL, and paste the address. On a Mac: File, then Follow a Show by URL.
- Overcast: the Open in Overcast button on the profile does it in one tap.
- Pocket Casts: paste the address into the search box, then follow the show that appears.
- Most other podcast apps have an Add by URL or RSS choice. Spotify cannot take private feeds.

The address is private to its member. If it gets out, Make a new address on the profile: the old one stops working and the member adds the new one to their app. Feed activity, when switched on (with a privacy policy line first), shows when each feed was last fetched and marks an address fetched from more than 5 networks in a week as Maybe shared.

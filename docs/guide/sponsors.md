# Sponsor messages

*Your own recorded messages before, in the middle of and after readings in the feed.*

Sponsor messages are short messages placed in readings in the podcast feed: a pre-roll, a mid-roll or a post-roll. The player on the post keeps the reading as it is. Every listener gets the same file, and nothing about listeners is used. Only administrators can make them.

## Making a message

1. Go to Foxfire Studio, Sponsor messages, Add New. The title is for your list only.
2. Under The message, fill in Who it is for (the name used in the episode notes and the chapter A word from...), Kind (Paid or gifted, or Your own), an optional Link, and the Script, one paragraph a line. Add Dates if it should run only for a while.
3. Under Where it can go, tick Pre-roll, Mid-roll, Post-roll or several, and under Which readings choose every reading, some categories or some readings. Save.
4. In the Recording box at the side, record it in the studio and press Use this recording there.
5. Publish the message to start it.

A paid or gifted message must say so near its start: the law in Canada and the US asks for it. The screen says whether it heard a word such as sponsored in the first seconds, as a reminder.

## Where messages go

Turn them on in Foxfire Studio, Podcast feed, Sponsor messages, Place sponsor messages in the feed. There, Mid-rolls sets the shortest reading that takes one and which kinds of reading may; a reading with the music bed never takes a mid-roll. Chapters gives each message a chapter listeners can skip. Episode notes sets the line at the start of the notes. Thank sponsors under the player adds a short line on the post. Members hear sponsor messages too decides member feeds. When two messages want the same place, the one with the lower Order (Post Attributes) wins.

Each message's Copies in each reading's sound and Where it runs list the episodes it is in now and where it has run. Copies are made again by themselves when a message or reading changes.

# Collections and audiobooks

*Several readings joined into one, and copies ready for Audible and other stores.*

## Making a collection

1. Go to Foxfire Studio, Collections, Add New, and give it a title and, if you like, some text and a featured image.
2. In Readings in this collection, search for each post with a reading and add it, in the order they should play. Choose the Gap between readings.
3. Tick In the podcast feed if it should be an episode, and choose Bonus or Full. Choose Who can hear this collection.
4. Save or publish. The readings are joined into one MP3 on the site, each copied exactly as it is on its post, with a chapter for each and their captions joined.

The collection has its own page with the player. If a reading changes, the collection is joined again by itself; if one is taken off its post, the collection keeps its file and says so until it is saved again.

## An audiobook

The Audiobook box on a saved collection makes one file for each reading to the rules of Audible (ACX), which Spotify for Authors, Authors Republic and Findaway also accept: room tone at each end, the right level and format, opening and closing credits, and a retail sample. Nothing of it is sent anywhere.

1. Under Opening credits and Closing credits, check the scripts, press Record in the studio, read them there and press Use for the audiobook.
2. Under Retail sample, choose the reading for the sample and where it starts (one to five minutes, starting with narration, nothing explicit).
3. Set the collection's featured image as the cover: square, 2400 by 2400 pixels or larger.
4. Press Make the audiobook. It downloads a ZIP with every file, the cover and a check report.

Each chapter uses the reading's audiobook copy when it has one (see Finish the sound), or is made from the post's reading. A reading with the music bed cannot be used, since the music fills the pauses where the room should be heard. The check report lists what passed and what ACX also asks that cannot be checked here.

> ACX takes an audiobook only for a book already on Amazon as a Kindle ebook, from a rights holder in the US, UK, Canada or Ireland. Listen through once for clicks, bumps or a word read twice before you upload.

# Keyboard shortcuts

*The studio can be used without a mouse.*

- Space or Page Down starts and pauses. Page Up goes back a sentence.
- Left and right arrows change the pace. Up and down step a paragraph.
- Enter while paused starts from the chosen word. P opens prompter view.
- Escape from any control goes back to the script. Press ? in the studio for the full list.

In the review: Delete cuts the selected words, Ctrl+Z undoes, and [ and ] move the start of a retake's splice by 10 ms (Shift+[ and Shift+] move its end).

If single-key shortcuts get in the way of a screen reader or voice control, switch them off under Controls, Reading, Single-key shortcuts. The buttons do the same things.
